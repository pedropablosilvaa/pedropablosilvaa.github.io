Welcome to my new blog!

19 Jan 2026

Welcome!

Recently, I made several changes to my personal website, mostly on the aesthetic side (it used to be pretty ugly… even more than it is now 😅), but I also made some changes so I can start publishing posts related to data.

I’ve always believed that knowledge should be shared. At least for me, it’s one of the most powerful ways to transform someone’s life—just like it happened to me—opening doors I never imagined, not even in my wildest dreams.

The motivation behind this website doesn’t come from ego, but from a simple need: to express myself and share what I’ve been learning over the years, thanks to all the people I’ve had the privilege to learn from, both at university and in the different jobs I’ve had throughout my short career.

The articles on this blog will focus on data engineering, artificial intelligence (in the broad sense of the word), and geospatial engineering.

And if you have any questions, thoughts, or comments, I’ll be happy to chat and respond.

Tepualia

Tepualia is a project under construction. The main idea is to share information related to the Chiloé Archipelago, with a focus on natural sciences and social sciences, allowing locals from Chiloé to rediscover their territory and helping tourists understand that the island is not just about landscapes, stilt houses, and curanto: it is an ancient society with fascinating traditions, unfolding among beautiful landscapes shaped by a unique biodiversity that has captivated scientists for decades.

Tepualia not only aims to help tourists understand the complexity of Chiloé, but we also want our fellow Chilotes to feel that sense of territorial belonging that many of us carry deep inside.

To enter to Tepualia please enter to www.tepualia.org
